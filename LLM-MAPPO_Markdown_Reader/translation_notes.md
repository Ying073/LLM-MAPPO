# Translation and extraction notes

- Source: selectable-text IEEE author-version PDF, 17 pages, two-column layout.
- Translation: manually produced Chinese translation with canonical terminology locked in the reader glossary. No external translation service was used for the completed reader.
- Cross-column and cross-page paragraphs were rejoined before translation. Publisher boilerplate and download-license footers were omitted because they are not paper content.
- Figures 1–11 and Tables I–II were cropped from the rendered source pages and placed beside their first substantive discussion with bilingual captions.
- Equations (1)–(29) were visually checked against page renders. Because the PDF text layer fragments fractions, subscripts, summations, and cases, the reader uses original equation crops as the authoritative representation rather than inventing uncertain LaTeX. `equations.md` provides a compact Chinese-purpose index.
- Reference entries remain in the original bibliographic language to preserve author names, titles, volume/issue/page fields, and DOI accuracy. Page images for pp.16–17 are linked from the References section.
- Author biographies are translated in blocks S065–S069.
- `source_map.json` is synchronized from the final Markdown anchors and records every S/F/T/E block used by the reader.
